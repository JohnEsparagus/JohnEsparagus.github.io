Cross-lingual hybrid search engine

  • C++20
  • Python
  • BM25 + dense

Built a search engine from scratch in C++ — inverted index, BM25 ranking, the actual math search engines use to decide relevance. Works across English, Chinese and Arabic, each with its own tokeniser.

It's now hybrid: BM25 for exact keyword matches, dense embeddings for meaning, and the two rankings fused together. The dense side is what makes it cross-lingual — you can search every book with one query in any language, which BM25 alone can't do since it needs the query and the text to share words.

Wanted to actually understand how "search" works under the hood instead of just using Elasticsearch and moving on.

Try it live — search English, Chinese and Arabic books side by side with BM25, dense and hybrid results.

NaviGo

  • FastAPI
  • Spatial database
  • UCL

Over the past year, I've had the opportunity to work on NaviGo, a university research project focused on improving accessibility through geospatial data and interactive mapping.

One of the most rewarding parts of the project was learning how a real software system comes together. We designed a full-stack application with a FastAPI backend, a reusable frontend widget, and a spatial database capable of storing and querying accessibility information efficiently. Working with concepts such as REST APIs, database design, spatial data modelling, and layered software architecture gave me experience that went far beyond coursework.

What I'll remember most, though, is the team. Working alongside talented teammates challenged me to think differently, ask better questions, and appreciate the value of clean engineering practices and collaboration. Seeing how different people approached problems — from backend architecture to frontend design — was a huge learning experience.

Looking back, I'm proud of what we built and grateful for everyone who contributed. It's exciting to see how much I've learned since the beginning of the project, and it has given me an even greater appreciation for building software that can have a real impact on people's lives.

A big thank you to my teammates, our supervisors, and everyone who supported us throughout the project. I'm looking forward to applying these lessons to future projects and continuing to grow as a software engineer.
