About

I'm Amir, a Computer Science student at UCL.

Most of what I build starts from wanting to know how something actually works. Search engines, shells, renderers — I'd rather write a small one from scratch than just use the finished thing and move on.

I spent a gap year travelling and writing a GPU particle renderer on a laptop with no dedicated GPU, which taught me more about performance than anything else I've done. Since then I've kept going down the graphics route with a CPU rasterizer and Vulkan.

The thing I care about most is language. I'm learning Arabic, built an app to help myself do it, and my search engine works across English, Chinese and Arabic. Long term I want to dedicate my career to removing language barriers through AI and software engineering.
